Use LostKorea in your AI app

MCP connection · 한국어

Connect the address below to an AI app such as Claude, and the AI can search items handed in to police stations and lost-and-found centers across Korea for you. MCP is the common standard AI apps use to call outside tools. No sign-up — just paste the address.

MCP address
https://lostkorea.com/mcp

Connect

  • Claude (web & desktop): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → name 'LostKorea', URL above. (Which plans can use it follows Claude's policy.)
  • Claude Code: one line in your terminal
    claude mcp add --transport http lostkorea https://lostkorea.com/mcp
  • Cursor and others: in the MCP config file
    { "mcpServers": { "lostkorea": { "url": "https://lostkorea.com/mcp" } } }
    Other apps: put the address in their 'remote (HTTP) MCP server URL' field.

What it can do

ToolWhat it does
find_matchesRuns lost-item reports (any language) through the same AI matcher as this site and returns candidates with scores, reasons, the holding agency, phone and the police listing. Reports are not saved.
search_found_itemsBrowse found items by keyword, area and date (like the 'All found items' page).
get_found_itemChecks whether one item is still held by the police ('보관중') or closed ('종결').

Try asking: "I lost a black card wallet on subway line 2 near Hongdae yesterday — find it with LostKorea", "Show AirPods found in Gangnam-gu", "Is item F2026111800003998 still at the police station?"

Limits and notes

  • Without a key: 3 AI matches per day (3 per call). Searching and status checks: 60 per hour.
  • Results are AI-picked candidates. Confirm with the holding agency by phone or in person.
  • Phone and card numbers in your text are masked before AI. See the Privacy Policy.

Organization keys

Organizations that handle lost items or help visitors can get a key for more use (10 per call, from 300 per day).

  • Who: police stations and police boxes; subway, rail, bus and airport lost-and-found centers; large venues such as theme parks and department stores; tourist information centers and interpretation hotlines; embassies and consulates; hotels and guesthouses; universities, research institutes and non-profits working on lost property.
  • Not eligible: individuals (please use the free 3 per day), and anyone reselling or bulk-collecting results.
  • Apply: email {{HELP_EMAIL}} from your organization email with the organization, contact, purpose and expected daily volume. We reply to that address with a key.
  • Conditions: results are candidates and owners confirm with the agency · no unnecessary personal data in reports · no redistribution or resale · keys may be revoked for misuse (Terms).
  • Using a key: add the header X-MCP-Key: your-key in your AI app's MCP settings. In Claude Code
    claude mcp add --transport http lostkorea https://lostkorea.com/mcp --header "X-MCP-Key: <key>"
